# Contributing to cntryl projects

Thank you for contributing. Repository-specific setup, architecture, and style
guidance takes precedence over this organization-wide guidance.

## Before starting

- Search existing issues and pull requests.
- Open an issue before beginning a large feature, architectural change, broad
  refactor, or compatibility change so maintainers can agree on scope.
- Small, focused corrections may be submitted directly.

## Pull requests

- Explain the problem, what changed, and why.
- Link related issues and keep the change focused.
- Call out important compatibility, security, migration, performance, or
  operational concerns.
- Add or update tests where appropriate and report only the validation you ran.

## Tool assistance

Disclose material AI or generative-tool assistance. Briefly name the kind of
tool, what it assisted, and how you reviewed its output. Routine completion,
formatting, spelling correction, and search do not need disclosure.

Do not include private prompts, credentials, proprietary material, or other
confidential information.

## Responsibility

By submitting a contribution, you take responsibility for the complete change.
Review it, understand it, distinguish verified results from assumptions, and be
prepared to answer questions or revise it. You must have the right to submit the
work under the repository's license.
